Your questions answered

How do I apply to French universities?

There's no common application system. Apply on each university's own website.

  • Applications usually run from October to February.
  • French-taught courses need a DELF B2 diploma (General, Student, Junior or Professional) or a DALF diploma (C1 or C2).
  • English-taught courses: check with each university whether your O-Level or A-Level English results are accepted.
  • Average annual fees: €3,000 to €20,000.
How does NS affect my application?

Group A (girls, and boys taking a scholarship with NS disruption, e.g. PSC): apply in JC2, before your A-Levels, to start the following year. Email the teachers in charge if you need help, e.g. with predicted grades.

Group B (NS boys): French universities don't usually let you apply after A-Levels (they're unfamiliar with NS), and expect you to apply in your graduating year (JC2).

  • Email the university in JC2 to ask for an exemption to apply 2 years later.
  • Copy the French Embassy (study@ambafrance-sg.org), as universities often check the Singapore grading system with them.
  • Sciences Po only lets you apply once, in JC2.
Do I need recommendation letters?
  • Only if your course asks for them. Some don't need any.
  • If you do, request at least 2 from your tutors by 30 September 2026, so they have time to write strong ones.
Where can I get more help with France?

The French Embassy offers free education counselling at Campus France, on most Thursday mornings at Alliance Française (Newton MRT). Give them notice by emailing study@ambafrance-sg.org or on WhatsApp at +65 8225 9549.

Where can I get more help with Japan?

The Embassy of Japan has a student advisor, Mr Ong Kong Leong, who gives free advice on studying in Japan.

  • He runs WhatsApp sessions 2 days a month. See the schedule (scroll to the bottom).
  • For an urgent reply, email studyinjapan@sn.mofa.go.sg.
