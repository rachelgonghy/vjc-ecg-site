The programme

What happens on the day

Every October, VJC welcomes JC1 students and their parents to hear how university admissions work and how higher education is changing.

Representatives from all six local autonomous universities share what they look for, the pathways they offer and the opportunities on campus. Before the panel, visit the university booths in the concourse for one-to-one conversations.

The Q&A is your chance to ask what you really want to know, so come with questions.
