Your questions answered

Why does the personal essay matter so much?

US colleges look at the whole person, not just grades. Your essay is where they hear your voice, so start early. Most strong essays go through many drafts.

Can I take the SAT or ACT more than once?

Yes. Unlike UK admissions tests, you can sit the SAT or ACT several times to reach your target score. Register early: registration closes about a month before each test.

What is Early Decision?

Early Decision lets you apply to one college earlier, usually in November, and hear back sooner. It is binding: if you get in, you're expected to accept. Regular Decision deadlines usually fall in early January, and some colleges set their own dates.

What do I need to do through VJC?
  • Request recommendation letters and indicate your interest on the VJC Portal.
  • Send the counsellor invitation from the Common App to the ECG committee (US section).
  • Attend the ECG briefing for US applicants in August.
