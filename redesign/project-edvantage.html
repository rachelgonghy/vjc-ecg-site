A mentor in your corner

At the heart of Project EdVantage is mentorship. You're paired with a dedicated teacher-mentor who works with you to:

  • Clarify your aspirations, strengths and goals
  • Build a roadmap for scholarship and university applications
  • Develop the skills and confidence for competitive selection

Beyond academics, the programme builds well-rounded students ready to make an impact in their chosen fields.
