What happens, and when

A recommended timeline. VJC's own dates always come first.

  1. Ongoing
    Research courses and universities. Start drafting your personal statement.
  2. JC1, end of year
    Shortlist up to 5 choices and check which admissions tests each one needs.
  3. JC2, June onwards
    VJC checkpoints begin for your UCAS form, references and predicted grades.
    VJC checkpointsSee dates
  4. JC2, July to October
    Sit admissions tests such as UCAT, LNAT, ESAT, TARA or TMUA. Register early.
  5. JC2, mid-October
    Medicine and Oxbridge applications close.
  6. JC2, December
    Interviews for Oxbridge and many Medicine courses.
  7. January
    Main deadline for most other courses. Offers arrive through the first half of the year.
  8. A-Level results
    Conditional offers are confirmed once your results are out.

Your questions answered

How does the UCAS form work?

You list up to 5 university and course choices on one form, and can submit only one form a year. Each course uses up one choice, even at the same university.

  • Oxford and Cambridge: one course only, and you can't apply to both.
  • LSE: picking more than one course isn't advised.

Universities see four parts:

  1. Personal particulars and course choices
  2. Education and results
  3. Personal statement
  4. School reference, written by VJC's UK Higher Education tutors

For the reference, request comments from your civics tutor, subject tutors and CCA teacher on the VJC Portal. Then list your CCAs and events.

What happens after I apply?

Each university replies with one of three outcomes:

  • Rejected
  • Conditional offer: your A-Level results must meet set grades
  • Unconditional offer

Check and reply to offers on UCAS Track. Once all your choices have replied, you can hold up to two: a Firm choice and an Insurance back-up. Decline the rest. If your Firm is unconditional, you can't hold an Insurance.

Miss the reply date and your offers are declined automatically. If every choice rejects you, you can still try Clearing for courses with places left.

Do I need to pay for my UCAS form?
  • Yes. VJC won't process your application until it's paid.
  • Paying doesn't submit your form. It can still come back to you for changes before the final internal deadline.
  • Applying to Cambridge Medicine? It has an earlier deadline, so check with the ECG team.
How does NS affect my application?
  • Some universities accept a deferred start for NS. To apply earlier, email the ECG team, who will open the Higher Education Module on the VJC Portal for you case by case.
  • You won't need predicted grades, as you'll have your A-Level results. Still request tutor comments on the Portal for your reference.
  • Take each round seriously. Applying "for practice" wastes tutors' time and can affect how universities view future VJC applicants.
