Which combinations are counted?

Your best 3 H2 subjects and H1 General Paper always count, out of 70. The calculator also tries adding your 4th content subject (a 4th H2 at half its points, or an H1), your Mother Tongue, or both, then scales each total back to 70.

You get whichever is highest, so an extra subject can only help.

Is this my official score?

No. It's an estimate to help you plan. Universities work out the official score from your results. Check each course's own requirements too.
